# Conventions

**Status: empty. To be filled by issue #3 before any calculation begins.**

This file is the single source of truth for notation in this repository. Conventions in the
$q$-deformed literature differ between sources — in the coproduct, in the normalisation of the
Casimir, in whether $q$ or $q^{1/2}$ appears, in the sign of the deformation parameter, and in the
labelling of representation series. Divergent conventions are the most common source of error in
this area, so they are fixed here once and cited from everywhere else.

Required contents, per issue #3:

1. **The quantum group.** $U_q(\mathfrak{su}(1,1))$: generators, relations, coproduct, counit,
   antipode, $*$-structure, and the convention for $q$ (real, $0 < q < 1$, versus $q = e^{-\lambda}$
   versus $\tilde q$ conventions in the DSSYK literature).
2. **The Casimir.** Its normalisation, and its relation to the DSSYK Hamiltonian.
3. **The normaliser extension** $\rtimes \mathbb{Z}_2$: what the $\mathbb{Z}_2$ acts as, and why the
   extension is needed (per arXiv:2512.10101).
4. **Representation series.** Principal, discrete, complementary and **strange** series: labels,
   ranges of labels, unitarity conditions, and the behaviour of each as $q \to 1$.
5. **The quantum homogeneous space** fixed by issue #5: coordinates, the $*$-algebra of functions,
   the invariant weight.
6. **DSSYK dictionary.** Chord number, length $\ell$, energy $E = \frac{2\cos\theta}{\sqrt{1-q}}$
   or whichever normalisation is adopted, and the map to representation labels.
7. **Translation table** between the conventions of each source actually used
   (arXiv:2512.10101, arXiv:2212.13668, arXiv:2412.19681, the Askey–Wilson literature,
   Masuda–Mimachi–Nakagami–Noumi–Ueno and successors for the strange series).

Every entry states the source it follows and every deviation from that source.
